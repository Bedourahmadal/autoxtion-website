Sectors

Solutions adapted to the requirements of different sectors.

Industry & Energy

Training in operating procedures, safety and emergency response, built on local regulatory requirements, inside a simulation of the site itself, without putting people or equipment at risk, and with a documented assessment of every trainee.

  • Equipment operation and operating procedures
  • Safety and emergency scenarios, built on local regulatory requirements
  • Automated assessment and performance reports

Safety & Security

Safety and emergency-response training based on local regulatory requirements, in a setting that mirrors the workplace, without exposing people or equipment to operational risk.

  • Safety and emergency scenarios
  • Every decision and its timing recorded, and turned into a clear report for the supervisor
  • Converting existing training material into interactive experiences

Education

For schools, universities and education authorities: immersive learning that complements the curriculum, works in the classroom and the lab, and can be accessed by students on their phones.

  • Immersive lessons built on the curriculum
  • Safe virtual labs and simulations
  • 3D models explored from every angle
  • AR experiences in the classroom and on the phone

Training & Professional Development

For training centers, we develop custom scenarios and simulations that turn a training package into hands-on practice, and offer their teams professional XR programs.

  • Converting existing training material into interactive experiences
  • Automated assessment and performance reports
  • Professional programs and hands-on workshops

Culture & Heritage

We design visitor experiences, digital guides and interactive artworks that tell the story of a place and answer its visitors’ questions.

  • Visitor and exhibition experiences
  • Digital guides and characters
  • Spatial storytelling and interactive artworks
  • Browser-based AR, with no app to install
