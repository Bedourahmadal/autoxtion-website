Interactive XR Experiences

We design AR, VR and MR experiences that add a digital layer to a place, an artwork or a product, with digital characters that tell stories and answer questions.

What it includes

  • Visitor and exhibition experiences
  • Digital guides and characters
  • Spatial storytelling and interactive artworks
  • Browser-based AR, with no app to install

Capabilities used

Augmented Reality
A digital layer over a place, an artwork or a machine, straight from a phone.
Interactive Content & Characters
Digital guides and characters that narrate, guide users and answer questions.
AI Integration
We develop digital guides and characters that hold a conversation with the user, where conversation adds a real function to the experience.
